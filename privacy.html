Privacy Policy

Organization: CLICKVIBE MEDIA • Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: All software applications developed by CLICKVIBE MEDIA operate entirely offline, request zero sensitive device permissions, and do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

1. Overview and Core Philosophy

CLICKVIBE MEDIA ("we", "our", or "the organization") designs and publishes standalone, offline-first utility applications. This Privacy Policy outlines the operational and technical framework governing all software applications released by CLICKVIBE MEDIA.

Our software is engineered to execute solely on the user's local device without relying on internet connectivity, external servers, or remote infrastructure.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CLICKVIBE MEDIA does not collect, record, process, transmit, or monetize any personal or sensitive user information. This policy applies strictly to all applications and services developed by our studio:

  • No personal identification data (such as names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses).
  • No unique device identifiers (including IMEI, Android ID, MAC address, or Advertising ID).
  • No network data, IP addresses, or connection parameters.
  • No geolocation or physical location metrics.
  • No usage statistics, session logs, or crash reporting telemetry.
  • No input data, calculations, notes, or files generated by users while operating the applications.

3. Local Processing and Storage Architecture

All computations, application logic, and functional operations take place strictly within the hardware environment of the user's local device. Applications do not establish connections to remote servers or cloud databases.

Any application preferences or user-generated content created during local sessions remain exclusively within the application's isolated local sandbox. This data is not accessible to the developer or any external third parties, and is completely purged when the application is uninstalled or when local application storage is cleared via device settings.

4. Zero-Permission Policy

Applications developed by CLICKVIBE MEDIA do not request sensitive operating system permissions. Our applications operate without:

  • Internet access or network state monitoring.
  • Precise or approximate geolocation access.
  • Camera or visual capture access.
  • Microphone or audio recording access.
  • Address book or contacts list access.
  • Shared or external storage access.

5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To ensure that user privacy remains uncompromised, our software applications operate without third-party integration libraries. Specifically, our applications do not contain:

  • Advertising mediation networks or commercial ad SDKs.
  • Analytics, behavioral tracking, or performance telemetry libraries.
  • Social media plugins or external tracking pixels.
  • Cloud databases or external payment processors.

6. Children's Privacy

Because our applications do not collect personal data of any description, operate entirely offline, and require no user registration, they pose zero privacy risk to individuals of any age, including children under the age of 13.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain full transparency. Any revisions will be published directly on this website with an updated revision date.
